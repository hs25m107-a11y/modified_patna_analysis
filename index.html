Study Overview

This study examines how residential population was distributed across Patna and how the population changed between 2000 and 2020. Google Earth Engine and the Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL) Population dataset were used to analyse population at 5-year intervals.

5

Discussion

Key finding: Patna's estimated population increased from 1,748,350 in 2000 to 2,507,450 in 2020, representing an overall increase of 759,100 people or 43.42%.

Spatial Pattern

The maps show that population is concentrated in the residential and built-up portions of Patna. The spatial distribution can be compared across the five-year intervals to identify areas where population concentration increased.

Temporal Pattern

The population increased consistently across all five-year observations: 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015 and 2020. The animation provides a visual representation of this change over time.

6

Methodology

Boundary: Patna urban local body boundary obtained from the Indian urban administrative boundaries dataset.
Population dataset: JRC Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL) Population, GHS_POP, at 100 m resolution.
Study period: 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015 and 2020.
Platform: Google Earth Engine with Python and geospatial visualization using Folium.
7

Limitations

  • GHSL provides gridded population estimates rather than household-level observations.
  • The analysis depends on the selected Patna administrative boundary.
  • Population estimates may differ from official census totals because of differences in data sources and estimation methods.
  • A 100 m grid represents population spatially but does not imply that population is evenly distributed within each cell.
8

Data Sources

GHSL Population: JRC Global Human Settlement Layer, GHS_POP, 2000–2020.

Administrative Boundary: Indian urban local body boundary dataset from the Indian Administrative Boundaries repository.

Analysis Platform: Google Earth Engine.
